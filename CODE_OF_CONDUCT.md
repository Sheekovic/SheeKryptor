# Code of Conduct

SheeKryptor is committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone.

Be respectful, assume good intent, accept constructive feedback, and focus discussion
on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and
publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. Report conduct concerns privately
to `sheekovic@gmail.com`. Reports will be reviewed with discretion and confidentiality.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.
